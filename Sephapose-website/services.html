Our Services

Sephapose Old Age Care Centre is committed to creating a safe, caring and supportive environment for elderly people and the wider community.

Our vision is to develop a modern old age care centre where elderly people can receive care, support, companionship and opportunities to enjoy their later years with dignity.

1. Elderly Care and Support

Our main focus is to provide compassionate care and support for elderly people. The planned Sephapose Old Age Care Centre will provide a safe and welcoming environment where seniors can feel respected, comfortable and valued.

The centre is intended to support the physical, emotional and social wellbeing of elderly residents while encouraging dignity, independence and community participation.

Planned elderly care services include:

  • Daily care and assistance
  • A safe and comfortable living environment
  • Social activities and companionship
  • Wellness and recreational activities
  • Support for families and caregivers
  • Community activities for seniors

2. Sustainable Community Garden

Sephapose is also involved in community gardening activities that encourage food production, participation and self-sustainability.

The community garden provides an opportunity for local community members to participate in growing food and learning about sustainable agriculture.

The garden currently requires additional farming equipment and resources to reach its full potential. Support from partners and donors could help us develop the garden further.

Community garden goals:

  • Encourage food production
  • Promote sustainable gardening
  • Support community participation
  • Create opportunities for learning
  • Improve food security within the community

3. Community Day Care Centre

Sephapose has also supported the community through a day care initiative for young children.

The day care initiative is intended to provide children with a safe environment where they can learn, play and interact with others while their parents and guardians attend to their daily responsibilities.

The facility is currently not operating while additional funding and resources are being secured to improve the children's environment and provide appropriate facilities.

Planned community day care support includes:

  • A safe environment for children
  • Learning and educational activities
  • Play and recreational activities
  • Social development
  • Community support for parents and guardians

4. Community Support and Outreach

Sephapose Old Age Care Centre believes that caring for elderly people is part of building a stronger community.

Through community activities and outreach, we aim to bring people together, provide support and create opportunities for members of the community to participate in meaningful activities.

Our community support activities include:

  • Community gatherings
  • Social activities
  • Support for elderly people
  • Community awareness activities
  • Volunteer opportunities
  • Partnership opportunities

Our Future Old Age Care Centre

Our long-term goal is to develop a modern old age care centre that will provide elderly people with a safe, dignified and comfortable place to live.

The proposed development will aim to include comfortable accommodation, healthcare support, recreational areas, gardens, communal spaces and activities designed specifically for elderly people.

We are looking for individuals, organisations, businesses, donors and partners who share our vision and would like to support the development of the new centre.

Help us build a better future for our elderly.

If you would like to support the project, make a donation, become a partner or learn more about our plans, please contact us.
