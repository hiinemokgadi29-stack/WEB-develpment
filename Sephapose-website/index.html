Welcome to Sephapose Old Age Care Centre

A place built on care, dignity, compassion and community.

Sephapose Old Age Care Centre is committed to creating a safe, caring and welcoming environment for elderly people.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide compassionate and respectful care to elderly people while creating an environment where they can live with dignity, comfort and purpose.


Our Vision

Our vision is to build a caring community where every elderly person feels valued, respected, safe and connected.



Our Values

Care We treat every person with kindness, patience and compassion.
Dignity We respect the individuality and dignity of every elderly person.
Respect We value every person and listen to their needs, experiences and wishes.
